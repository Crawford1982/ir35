About

About ir35guide.co.uk

Last updated: October 2026

Quick summary: ir35guide.co.uk is an independent UK contractor tax resource built by James Crawford. All content is based on published HMRC guidance and UK tax legislation. This site provides general information only — it is not professional tax advice.

Author & Publisher

James Crawford is the author and publisher of ir35guide.co.uk. He built this site and its calculators to provide UK contractors with free, accessible information about IR35 and contractor tax rules.

Content & Sources

All content on this site is based on:

  • Published HMRC guidance and official government resources
  • Current UK tax legislation (Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, Finance Acts, and related statutory instruments)
  • Publicly available tax rates, thresholds, and allowances from HMRC.gov.uk

Tax rates, National Insurance thresholds, and legislative changes are reviewed and updated regularly to reflect current UK tax years.

Review & Updates

Content and calculators are reviewed and updated throughout the year, with particular attention given to:

  • April tax year changes (new rates, thresholds, and IR35 rule changes)
  • Budget announcements and Finance Act updates
  • HMRC guidance updates and case law developments

This page was last updated in October 2026.

Not Professional Tax Advice

This site provides general information only and is not professional tax, legal, or financial advice. The calculators provide estimates based on standard tax rules — your individual circumstances may differ.

Always consult a qualified accountant or tax adviser for advice specific to your situation. This is especially important for:

  • IR35 status assessments and contract reviews
  • Complex tax scenarios (multiple income sources, non-standard expenses, etc.)
  • HMRC investigations or disputes
  • Business structuring and tax planning decisions
